# Real-Estate Dependency Audit

## Scope and method

Audited runtime Python under `app/` and `scripts/`, excluding `tests/`. Searched case-insensitively for `handover`, `GDV`, `HBU`, `hurdle`, `LAND*FEASIBILITY`, `construction`, and `delivery constraints`; separately searched for `re_*` domain identifiers. Matches are grouped by source location/function when they share the same behavior; each matching source line and term is listed. Thus, every matched line is accounted for, while related matches share a current-behavior and replacement description.

**Result:** 109 matching source lines across 9 runtime files for the business terms. No `re_*` domain identifiers or matching business terms were found in `scripts/`. Settings currently have no hardcoded `re_*` domain defaults. Test fixtures are intentionally excluded and not inventoried here.

Line references refer to the current workspace snapshot and may need refreshing after edits.

## Classification key

1. **Hardcoded KPI** — fixed KPI names, field aliases, or domain-specific KPI extraction/rendering.
2. **Hardcoded Risk** — fixed risk/cause/outcome language or domain-specific risk mapping.
3. **Hardcoded Action** — fixed mitigation, recommendation, prioritization, or deferral.
4. **Hardcoded Business Rule** — fixed decision logic, causal assumptions, thresholds, or keyword-based classification.
5. **Hardcoded Dashboard Taxonomy** — fixed dashboard category, theme, KPI/risk/action group, or taxonomy signals.
6. **Metadata Only** — sample, normalization, or documentation text that does not itself drive a decision.

## Coupling summary by requested area

- **Dashboard taxonomy assumptions:** `dashboard_intelligence_service.py` has built-in `LAND_FEASIBILITY` and `PROJECT_DELIVERY` definitions/signals; `scenario_intent_service.py` and `strategy_selector_node.py` repeat keyword/category routing.
- **KPI assumptions:** development-option aliases, HBU/GDV/IRR/hurdle/zoning field extraction, handover/status aliases, context whitelists, and specialized report renderers are in `dashboard_intelligence_service.py`, `explainability_service.py`, and `report_service.py`.
- **Root-cause assumptions:** `causal_reasoning_service.py` constructs a fixed operational delay chain; `root_cause_analysis_service.py` overrides evidence-derived causes and consequences for construction/handover scenarios.
- **Risk assumptions:** fixed delivery/customer/revenue/cashflow risks appear in the causal chain and root-cause overrides; dashboard taxonomy also declares real-estate/project risk groups.
- **Action assumptions:** dashboard taxonomy declares schedule recovery/resource actions; root-cause analysis injects a critical-path recovery action; financial reasoning recommends deferral/funding priority and critical-path recovery based on fixed assumptions.
- **`re_*` assumptions:** none found in current runtime Python. Current [app/config/settings.py](./app/config/settings.py) has no real-estate domain-ID default. This is a result for the scanned snapshot, not a guarantee about deployment environment variables.
- **Metadata-only hits:** the API sample query, HBU display normalization, and `rank_development_options` docstring are not decision rules by themselves. They remain domain-branded documentation/output normalization and should be made neutral or registry-driven as cleanup.
